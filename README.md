# Future Research Ideas

A repository to capture, organize, and develop future research ideas across various disciplines.

## Purpose

This repository serves as a personal research idea repository where you can:
- 📝 Document research ideas in a structured format
- 🗂️ Organize ideas by theme/category
- 📚 Maintain references and related work
- ❓ Track research questions and methodologies
- 🚀 Progress ideas from preliminary concepts to actionable research

## Structure

```
future-research-ideas/
├── README.md                      # This file
├── RESEARCH_IDEA_TEMPLATE.md      # Template for new ideas
├── ideas/
│   ├── ai_machine_learning/
│   ├── biology/
│   ├── physics/
│   ├── computer_science/
│   └── interdisciplinary/
└── archived/                      # Completed or archived ideas
```

## How to Use This Template

### Creating a New Research Idea

1. **Copy the template**: Use `RESEARCH_IDEA_TEMPLATE.md` as a starting point
2. **Name your file**: Create a new file with a descriptive name: `ideas/[category]/[idea_title].md`
3. **Fill in the sections**: Complete each section with relevant information
4. **Keep it concise**: Start simple and expand as the idea develops
5. **Update status**: Track progress from "Preliminary" to "Completed"

### Template Sections Overview

| Section | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Basic Information** | Title, category, date, and current status |
| **Overview** | High-level summary of the idea |
| **Research Question(s)** | Specific questions your research will address |
| **Motivation & Significance** | Why this research matters |
| **Key Concepts & Scope** | Core theories and research boundaries |
| **Preliminary Hypothesis** | Expected outcomes or initial assumptions |
| **Research Methodology** | Approach and methods you'll use |
| **References & Related Work** | Citations and related research |
| **Challenges & Open Questions** | Potential obstacles and unknowns |
| **Resources & Requirements** | Time, budget, skills, collaborators |
| **Next Steps** | Actionable items to move forward |
| **Notes & Ideas** | Brainstorming and evolving thoughts |

## Status Tracking

Track your research ideas through different stages:
- 🔵 **Preliminary**: Initial concept, needs development
- 🟡 **Developing**: Being actively refined, research questions clarified
- 🟢 **Ready for Research**: Well-defined, ready to execute
- ⏸️ **On Hold**: Paused for external reasons
- ✅ **Completed**: Research concluded or published

## Categories

Organize ideas into categories (create as needed):
- `ai_machine_learning/` - AI, ML, and related topics
- `biology/` - Biological sciences research
- `physics/` - Physics and related fields
- `computer_science/` - CS theory and applications
- `interdisciplinary/` - Cross-disciplinary research

## Tips for Effective Use

✅ **Do**:
- Start with rough ideas and refine them over time
- Link related ideas and cross-reference them
- Review and update ideas periodically
- Use checklists to track progress

❌ **Don't**:
- Wait for a perfect idea before documenting it
- Abandon incomplete ideas—archive them instead
- Ignore connections between ideas
- Forget to update your status
